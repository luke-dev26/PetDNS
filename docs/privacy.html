Privacy Policy

PetDNS is built with user privacy and security in mind. For full details about how we handle your data, please see the sections below.

Last updated: May 9, 2026

PetDNS (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how the App handles information required for its operation.

Data collection

PetDNS does not collect, transmit, or store personal data from your device. The App does not include advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, or other third-party services that receive user information.

API key storage

To use PetDNS, you provide your NextDNS API key. This key is stored only on your device using Apple’s secure on-device storage. It is not transmitted to any server controlled by the app developer.

Data displayed in the app

PetDNS connects directly to the NextDNS API from your device to fetch and display account data such as logs and settings. That data is retrieved on demand and remains under your control. The app developer does not receive or store your NextDNS account data.

NextDNS

PetDNS is a third-party application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NextDNS. For information about how NextDNS handles your data, please review the NextDNS Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
