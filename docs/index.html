Built for empowerment

More powerful profile management.

PetDNS empowers you to block trackers, monitor suspicious queries, and fine-tune security across multiple profiles for a safer, ad-free network. Built as a fully native app, it delivers powerful DNS log search, deep query analytics, and seamless allowlist and denylist management, along with a customizable dashboard and dynamic widgets.

  • Review live query activity to block unwanted domains as they appear.
  • Block a domain straight from your browser or copy your denylist from one profile to another using Shortcuts.
  • View detailed query analytics across multiple profiles.
  • Keep an eye on your network with home screen widgets.
Why PetDNS

Everything you need to monitor, optimize, and secure your network.

Query log visibility

Powerfull DNS log search with extensive filtering shows you exactly what is happening on your network and on your devices. View logs across multiple profiles or deeply investigate issues with filters for devices and block reason.

Fine-tuned management

Adjust everything from blocklists and denylists to advanced settings like Native Tracking Prevention, DNS Rewrites, and Parental Controlls all from the app.

Automation

Use Siri and Shortcuts to create your own analytics reports or build your own automations for the workflows that matter to you.

The PetDNS Promise

Managing your privacy should be easy and accessible. PetDNS promises to provide all profile management features available on the NextDNS website, including data retrieval, viewing logs, and managing settings for all of your individual profiles, in the app at no cost. No paywall will ever prevent you from seeing your data.
