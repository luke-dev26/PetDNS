Support and Feedback

PetDNS collects no tracking data from the app. Feedback is the best way to suggest improvements or report issues.

GitHub

The best place to report bugs, request features, or discuss a problem is the project issue tracker. Public issues make it easier to track and fix problems in a way that helps everyone.

Reddit

If you prefer to reach out without a GitHub account, you can contact me on Reddit at u/Cold-Weight951.

Email

For direct contact, email me at PetDNSApp@Outlook.com. This is typically the fastest way to reach me.

PetDNS values your privacy and collects no tracking data from the app. Without tracking data, there is no automated feedback showing what features you use most or what might be broken. I cannot stress enough how much your feedback means to me and how much I value your suggestions to any future features or improvements.

Thank you for using PetDNS!
